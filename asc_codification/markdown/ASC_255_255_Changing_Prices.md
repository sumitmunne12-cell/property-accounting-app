##### · · > Trading Account Investments in Fixed-income Securities Owned by Banks, Investment Brokers, and Others

255-10-55-2

Trading account securities are securities of all types carried in a trading account that are held principally for resale. Trading account investments include both fixed-income securities (for example, nonconvertible preferred stock, convertible bonds, and other bonds) and other securities (for example, common stock). Usually, trading account securities are held for extremely short periods of time—sometimes for only a few hours. Frequently, the entity buys and sells the securities expecting to make a profit on the difference between dealer and retail, or bid and ask, prices rather than on price changes during the period securities are held. However, the prices of the securities change with market forces.
