### > The Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974

962-10-05-8

Defined contribution pension plans are generally subject to certain of the reporting and other requirements of The Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974. In addition to establishing certain minimum standards for participation, vesting, and funding for employee benefit plans of private entities, the Act also requires annual reporting of certain information to particular governmental agencies and summarized information to plan participants. For many plans, the reporting requirements include financial statements prepared in conformity with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP).

#### Liquidation

The process by which an entity converts its assets to cash or other assets and settles its obligations with creditors in anticipation of the entity ceasing all activities. Upon cessation of the entity's activities, any remaining cash or other assets are distributed to the entity's investors or other claimants (albeit sometimes indirectly). Liquidation may be compulsory or voluntary.
